What's included

Masonry repairs, top to bottom.

Water is behind most chimney damage. We fix what's broken and deal with how the water got in.

01

Tuckpointing

Crumbling mortar joints ground out and repointed with new mortar matched to the old.

02

Brick replacement

Cracked, loose, or flaking bricks cut out and replaced with matching brick.

03

Crown repair

Cracked crowns sealed or rebuilt with a proper slope and drip edge, so water runs off the top.

04

Flashing

Loose or failed flashing where the chimney meets the roof repaired, resealed, or replaced.

05

Waterproofing

A breathable water repellent applied to sound masonry, so rain stays out and moisture inside can still escape.

06

Firebox repair

Cracked firebrick replaced and joints repointed with heat-rated refractory mortar.

07

Smoke chamber

Rough, gapped smoke chambers smoothed and sealed with a heat-rated coating for better draft.

08

Partial rebuilds

When the top of the chimney is too far gone to repair, it is rebuilt from the roofline up.

Your options

Seal, repair, or rebuild.

We'll show you photos of the damage and recommend the smallest repair that will actually last.

Option 01

Seal & protect

For a chimney that's sound but starting to take on water. Hairline crown cracks sealed, flashing resealed, and the masonry treated with a water repellent.

  • Sound brick and mortar
  • Stops early leaks
  • Least cost
Option 02

Repair & repoint

For worn mortar joints, a few damaged bricks, or a cracked crown. Joints repointed, bricks replaced, and the crown rebuilt.

  • Crumbling mortar
  • Flaking or loose bricks
  • New crown
Option 03

Rebuild from the roofline

For a chimney top that's leaning, loose, or badly weathered. The damaged section is taken down and rebuilt with new brick, crown, and cap.

  • Leaning or loose top
  • New brick, crown, and cap
  • Longest lasting

FAQ

Chimney repair questions.

Anything else, ask us when you book.

01Why is my chimney leaking?

The usual causes are a cracked crown, a missing or damaged cap, failed flashing where the chimney meets the roof, and worn mortar joints that soak up rain. We find the source first, because sealing the wrong spot doesn't stop the leak.

02What is the white staining on my chimney?

It's called efflorescence: salts left behind when water moves through the brick and evaporates. The stain itself is harmless, but it tells you the chimney is taking on water.

03Can masonry repairs be done in winter?

Some can. Mortar needs to cure above freezing and out of the rain, so larger masonry jobs are usually scheduled for drier weather. An urgent leak can be covered and protected until then.

04My chimney was damaged in an earthquake. Can it be repaired?

Often, yes. It starts with a Level 2 inspection to see how far the damage goes. See how inspections work.

05Is waterproofing just a coat of sealant?

No. Ordinary sealers and paint trap moisture inside the brick, which makes damage worse. A breathable water repellent keeps rain out while letting moisture escape.
