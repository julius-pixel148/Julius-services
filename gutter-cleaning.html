Your options

Once, twice a year, or guarded.

How often your gutters need cleaning depends mostly on the trees around your house.

Option 01

One-time cleaning

A full cleaning whenever you need it: before winter rain, before a home sale, or when the gutters start to overflow.

  • Gutters and downspouts
  • Roof blown off
  • Before and after photos
Option 02

Twice a year

The schedule most Northwest homes need: once in late fall after the leaves drop, and once in spring after the winter storms.

  • Late fall and spring
  • Set it and forget it
  • Problems caught early
Option 03

Gutter guards

Guards keep leaves and most needles out, so gutters flow longer between cleanings. They still need a check from time to time.

  • Less buildup
  • Fewer cleanings
  • Good for homes under trees

FAQ

Gutter questions.

Anything else, ask us when you book.

01How often should gutters be cleaned around Seattle?

At least twice a year for most homes: in late fall once the leaves are down, and again in spring. Homes under fir, cedar, or big-leaf maple often need a third visit.

02What happens if I skip it?

Clogged gutters overflow. The water runs down the siding and behind the fascia, rots the wood, and pools at the foundation, where it can find its way into a crawl space or basement.

03Do gutter guards mean I never clean my gutters again?

No. Guards cut the buildup a great deal, but fine debris and moss still get through, and needles can mat on top. Plan on a check every year or two.

04Do I need to be home?

Usually not. As long as we can reach all sides of the house and an outdoor water spigot, we can do the work and send you the photos.
