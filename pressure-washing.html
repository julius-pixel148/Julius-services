Methods

Pressure wash, soft wash, or wood care.

The method matters more than the machine. Here is how we match it to the surface.

Method 01

Pressure washing

High-pressure water for hard surfaces that can take it. A surface cleaner keeps the result even across large areas.

  • Concrete and stone
  • Driveways, walks, patios
  • Even, stripe-free finish
Method 02

Soft washing

Low pressure and a cleaning solution that does the work, then a gentle rinse. For surfaces that high pressure would damage.

  • Siding and trim
  • Painted surfaces
  • Kills algae at the root
Method 03

Wood care

Wide spray, low pressure, and careful technique for decks and fences, so the wood comes clean without raised grain or gouges.

  • Decks and fences
  • Low pressure
  • Ready for stain or sealer

FAQ

Pressure washing questions.

Anything else, ask us when you book.

01Can pressure washing damage my home?

It can if it's done with too much pressure or the wrong tip. High pressure gouges wood, strips paint, and forces water behind siding. That's why we soft wash siding and use low pressure on wood.

02Do you use my water?

Yes. We connect to an outdoor spigot at your home. Let us know when you book if you don't have one that works.

03How often should driveways and walkways be washed?

Once a year keeps most concrete clean around here. Shaded areas that stay damp grow algae faster and may need it more often.

04Do I need to be home?

Usually not, as long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and an outdoor water spigot. Please move cars and patio furniture beforehand if you can.
