FAQ

Questions.

Anything else, ask us when you book.

01How often should a chimney be swept?

The national standard, NFPA 211, calls for chimneys, fireplaces, and vents to be inspected at least once a year and cleaned when needed. If you burn wood regularly, plan on a sweep every year. More about chimney sweeping.

02When is the best time to book chimney work?

Late spring through early fall. The weather is dry enough for roof and masonry work, and you'll be ready before the first cold night. Fall is the busiest season, so booking early helps.

03How often do gutters need cleaning around Seattle?

At least twice a year for most homes: in late fall after the leaves drop, and again in spring. Homes under fir, cedar, or maple trees often need a third cleaning.

04Do you pressure wash roofs?

Not asphalt shingle roofs. High pressure strips the granules that protect the shingles. Moss is removed with gentler methods and then treated. See how moss removal works.

05Can you do several services in one visit?

Often, yes. A chimney sweep, gutter cleaning, and dryer vent cleaning fit well together, and it saves you a second appointment. Tell us everything you'd like done when you book.

06Do I need to be home?

For chimney, fireplace, and dryer vent work, yes, because we need to get inside. For gutters, roofs, and pressure washing, usually not, as long as we can reach the outside of the house and a water spigot.

07Which areas do you serve?

Seattle, Bellevue, Mercer Island, Kirkland, Redmond, Sammamish, Issaquah, Bothell, Renton, Shoreline, and the neighborhoods around them. See all service areas.
