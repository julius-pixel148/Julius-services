FAQ

Roof & moss questions.

Anything else, ask us when you book.

01Why is moss bad for a roof?

Moss holds moisture against the roofing and its roots work under the edges of shingles, lifting them. That lets water in and wears the roof out years early.

02Do you pressure wash roofs?

Not asphalt shingle roofs. High pressure strips off the granules that protect the shingles. Moss on shingles is removed with gentler methods and then treated. Some tile and metal roofs can be washed at low pressure.

03How long does moss treatment last?

It depends on shade, trees, and which way the roof faces. A sunny roof may stay clear for several years. A shaded north-facing roof under trees may need attention every year or two.

04When is the best time for moss removal?

Dry weather makes roof work safer and helps treatments work, so late spring through early fall is ideal. If moss is already lifting shingles, don't wait for the perfect season.
